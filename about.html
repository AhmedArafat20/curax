About CuraX

ONE TEAM ACROSS MARKETING, WEB, SOFTWARE AND AI.

Who we are

A digital partner, not a vendor.

CuraX was formed around a simple observation: businesses were hiring one agency for ads, another for a website, and a freelancer for software — and paying the cost of that disconnect in translation, delay and inconsistent results. We put marketing, web development, software and AI under one roof so a single team owns the outcome from strategy through to the system running in production.

Our Vision

A future where marketing and technology are planned as one discipline.

Our Mission

Build the systems that let businesses grow with less friction — and fewer handoffs.

What we do

Marketing

Paid, organic and lifecycle campaigns tied to measurable outcomes.

Web Development

Websites and web applications built for speed and clarity.

Software

CRMs, dashboards and internal tools shaped around real workflows.

AI

Automation and assistants applied where they save real time.

Our Philosophy

Good design and good engineering are the same discipline pointed at different materials. We hold campaign creative and product interfaces to the same standard, and we treat every technical decision as something a client should be able to understand, not just trust.

Why We Exist

Because most businesses don't need more vendors — they need fewer gaps between the people making decisions and the people building the thing. CuraX is built to close that gap.
